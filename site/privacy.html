privacy

privacy, plainly.

short version: this site has no analytics, no cookies, and no telemetry. the app keeps your vault on your mac and asks the network for very little.

this site

no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies. no third-party scripts and no webfonts — the page and its assets are served from this domain.

the site keeps two preferences in your browser's local storage: theme and accent. they never leave your machine, and clearing site data removes them.

the app

napkin++ has no account, no sync, and no telemetry. the vault is a plain JSON file under ~/Library/Application Support/napkin++/ on your mac. it is not uploaded anywhere.

the app only makes a network request when you:

  • add or refresh a link;
  • capture a link's preview or social image;
  • run stack detection from a live url or repository;
  • check for updates.

those requests go to the url or repository you gave it. update checks ask the release host whether a newer version exists. no usage data is attached.

the vault is not encrypted at rest in 1.0. it protects against the one-giant-text-file problem, not against someone with access to your unlocked mac. turn on FileVault. encryption at rest is the next milestone.

the contact form

the form stores the name, email, topic and message you send, so i can reply. it is not used for a mailing list, not sold, and not shared. ask and it gets deleted.

the form is delivered through Resend, an email service; it processes the message in order to send it to the inbox.

if the form can't send, your mail app opens with the same content. that path is email, handled by your provider and mine.

tips

tips go through Cash App, Venmo, or PayPal. those services handle the payment and their own privacy terms; i don't see your card details.

questions

email hello@napkinplusplus.app and ask. no legal department, no ticket number.
